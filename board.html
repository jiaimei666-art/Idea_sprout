How to grow ideas

  1. Plant: Pick a size, then click empty space for a sticky note.
  2. Tag: Type a tag, then press Enter or comma to make flair.
  3. Move: Drag a note by its dotted handle.
  4. Connect: Choose Connect, then click two notes.
  5. Draw: Use Pen, Line, Box, Circle, or Eraser. The size slider changes thickness.

Clear the whole board?

This removes every note, connection, and doodle.
